# Arena artwork

`enchanted_arena.png` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the user's selected
"Colorful magical adventure" direction. It is bundled locally for offline play.
The characters, spell icons and UI are drawn in Pygame and are not baked into this backdrop.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background for a 2D colorful magical adventure game, no UI. Create a polished hand-painted whimsical enchanted forest arena viewed from a steep overhead three-quarter angle, rich jewel teal and emerald foliage, violet flowering trees, warm golden sunlight, carved pale stone courtyard, charming ancient stone arches and mossy ruins framing the outer edges. The central 65 percent of the image must be a large OPEN and relatively uncluttered flat stone/moss playfield with subtle circular carved patterns, for moving game characters. A small softly glowing turquoise summoning circle is centered horizontally near 77 percent image height, leaving a clear area above for approaching enemies. Wide 4:3 landscape composition with corners and edges lush and richly detailed, coherent smooth painterly shading and beautiful atmospheric depth, sophisticated playful adventure-game art, readable bright midtones rather than dark moody fantasy. No characters, no creatures, no text, no lettering, no HUD, no logos, no watermarks, no border. Camera and perspective must allow this to be used as a playable top-to-bottom arena.
